## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer retention, churn patterns, customer lifetime value (CLV), and customer cohorts for a subscription-based business.

The goal is to understand customer behavior, identify higher-churn segments, compare subscription plans, and recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

## Objectives

- Calculate overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Compare retention across customer segments and subscription plans.
- Analyze customer cohorts by signup month.
- Calculate customer lifetime value (CLV).
- Identify churn patterns and potential retention drivers.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.

- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
